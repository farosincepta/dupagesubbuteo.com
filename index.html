Who we are

A Subbuteo club for Woodridge

Woodridge Table Soccer Club plays Subbuteo, the table football game where you flick small figures to pass, shoot and save, in Woodridge, Illinois.

We are a private sports team based in Woodridge, IL, established on October 6, 2026. We honor our community and the most beautiful soccer table top game. Beginners are welcome, and we teach the game to anyone who wants to learn.

Our home

We honor Woodridge

Woodridge is a village in Woodridge, Illinois, incorporated on August 24, 1959, on the wooded high ground above the East Branch of the DuPage River. It is our community, and our club carries its name. Find out what's happening in the village on its official website.
